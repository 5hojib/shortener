How it works

Three steps. Your visitor experiences a smooth flow, you earn on every visit.

  1. 01

    Visitor clicks your short link

    They land on Step 1 — a 10-second interstitial page where your first ad is shown.

  2. 02

    They continue to Step 2

    After 10 seconds, the Continue button unlocks. They proceed to a second 10-second page for another ad impression.

  3. 03

    Go button unlocks the destination

    After the second timer, the Go button appears and redirects them to your original URL.
